One sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving
planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment about
the food.

Entry for the [One Star, Five Stars for the Food](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7)
bounty on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output`) and self-checks the constraint: exactly one sentence,
at most 200 characters.

## Entry

> One star: your gravity bruised all three of my knees, the locals queue for hours to stare at glowing rectangles, yet your noodles alone almost make the abduction worth it.
